# Privacy Policy for Defense Tycoon

**Last Updated:** June 3, 2026

## Introduction

Defense Tycoon ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. **Locally Stored Data**
- **Game Progress**: Your oil, resources, buildings, units, and prestige points are stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage).
- **Settings**: Your language preference, sound/music settings, and game preferences are stored locally.
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do NOT transmit your game data to any server. All data remains on your device.

### 2. **Device Information**
- We do NOT collect device identifiers, IMEI, or any personally identifiable information.
- The app does NOT require account creation or login.

### 3. **Analytics & Crash Reporting**
- The app does NOT use analytics services, Google Analytics, or crash reporting tools.
- We do NOT track user behavior or gameplay metrics.

### 4. **Advertisements**
- The app may display advertisements through AdMob or similar ad networks.
- Ad networks may collect non-personally identifiable information (NPII) such as:
  - Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
  - Approximate location (country/region level)
  - Device type and OS version
- Please refer to Google's privacy policy for AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Security

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to or control over your personal information. The security of your data depends on your device's security.

## Third-Party Services

### Google Play Store
- Publishing through Google Play means your app download and installation are managed by Google.
- Google may collect basic metadata about your device (OS version, device type).
- See: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Ad Networks (AdMob)
- Ads are served by Google AdMob, which follows Google's privacy practices.
- You can control ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

## Children's Privacy

Defense Tycoon is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for all ages. The app does NOT knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

## Your Rights

- **Data Access**: All your game data is stored locally. You can access it anytime.
- **Data Deletion**: Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage will delete all local data.
- **Opt-Out**: You can disable personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
