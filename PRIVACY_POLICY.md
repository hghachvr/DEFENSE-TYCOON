# Privacy Policy for Defense Tycoon

**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Overview

Defense Tycoon ("App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Game Data (Stored Locally on Your Device)
- **Game Progress**: Your oil, resources, buildings, units, prestige points, and game state
- **Player Settings**: Language preference, sound/music settings, gameplay preferences
- **Save Data**: All data is stored **locally on your device** using browser storage (localStorage)

**Important**: We do NOT send your game data to any server. Everything stays on your device.

### 1.2 Device Information
- We do NOT collect device identifiers, IMEI, phone numbers, or personal contact information
- The App does NOT require account creation, login, or email
- We do NOT track your location

### 1.3 Analytics & Telemetry
- We do NOT use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any analytics services
- We do NOT collect gameplay metrics or user behavior data
- We do NOT track how you play or your progression

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Since we don't collect personal information, there's nothing to use or share. Your game data:
- Stays on your device
- Is only used to save/load your game
- Is never transmitted to us or third parties

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

### 3.1 Google Play Store
- Your app download is managed by Google Play
- Google may collect basic metadata (device type, OS version, app installation)
- See: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 3.2 GitHub Pages (Web Hosting)
- The game runs on GitHub Pages
- GitHub may collect standard web traffic data (IP, referrer, browser type)
- See: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

### 3.3 Advertisements (if enabled)
- The App may display ads through **Google AdMob** or similar services
- Ad networks collect:
  - Ad impressions and clicks
  - Approximate location (country/region level only)
  - Device type and OS version
- Ad networks do NOT have access to your game data
- See: https://policies.google.com/privacy for Google AdMob

---

## 4. Data Security

**All your game data is stored locally on your device.** We cannot access, modify, or delete it. Your device's security protects your data:
- Use device screen lock
- Keep your OS updated
- Don't install apps from untrusted sources

**Uninstalling the App or clearing app data will permanently delete all game saves.**

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Defense Tycoon is designed to be **family-friendly and suitable for all ages**. We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act):
- We do NOT knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13
- We do NOT require parental consent for using the App
- The App contains NO chat, NO user accounts, NO personal information collection

If we discover a child has provided personal information, we will delete it immediately.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Control

### 6.1 Data Access
- All your game data is stored locally on your device
- You can access it anytime by playing the game
- You can export your save by exporting your browser data

### 6.2 Data Deletion
- **Uninstall the App** → all local data is deleted
- **Clear browser storage** → all saves are deleted
- There's nothing on our servers to delete (everything is local)

### 6.3 Ad Personalization
- You can disable personalized ads in your device's Google settings
- Go to: Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy

### 6.4 Do Not Track (DNT)
- We respect Do Not Track signals, though we don't track anyway
- The App does NOT use cookies or tracking pixels

---

## 7. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally:
- **Game saves**: Stored until you uninstall the App or delete the data
- **Settings**: Stored until you uninstall or clear data
- **Server logs**: GitHub may retain access logs per their policy

---

## 8. International Users (GDPR, CCPA Compliance)

Defense Tycoon complies with:
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) - EU/UK users
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California users
- **LGPD** (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados) - Brazil users

Since we don't collect personal data, GDPR/CCPA rights (access, deletion, portability) don't apply to us, but:
- Your **game data is your property** and stored locally on your device
- You have **full control** to delete it anytime
